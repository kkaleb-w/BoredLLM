## Boredom

- **Eastwood, J. D., Frischen, A., Fenske, M. J., and Smilek, D. (2012).** The Unengaged
  Mind: Defining Boredom in Terms of Attention. *Perspectives on Psychological Science*, 7(5),
  482 to 495. https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691612456044
- **Bench, S. W., and Lench, H. C. (2013).** On the Function of Boredom. *Behavioral
  Sciences*, 3(3), 459 to 472. https://www.mdpi.com/2076-328X/3/3/459
- **Raffaelli, Q., Mills, C., and Christoff, K. (2016).** The knowns and unknowns of boredom:
  a review of the literature. https://www.christofflab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Raffaelli2016-boredom.pdf
- **Westgate, E. C. (2020).** Why Boredom Is Interesting. *Current Directions in
  Psychological Science*, 29(1), 33 to 40. https://doi.org/10.1177/0963721419884309
- **Boredom, Information-Seeking and Exploration** (Princeton, Daw lab). Boredom rises in
  environments with little useful information; higher boredom correlates with higher
  exploration. https://www.princeton.edu/~ndaw/gwdc16.pdf
- **Wilson, T. D., and colleagues (2014).** Just think: The challenges of the disengaged
  mind. *Science*. 43% of participants self-administered electric shocks rather than sit
  alone with their thoughts.
- **Danckert, J., and Merrifield, C. (2016).** Default mode network activation during boredom
  (imaging work contrasting boredom, resting state, and sustained attention).
- **Danckert, J., and Eastwood, J. D. (2020).** *Out of My Skull: The Psychology of Boredom.*
  Harvard University Press.
- **The Bored Brain: Insular Cortex and the Default Mode Network** (review of boredom, DMN,
  and mind-wandering overlaps). https://docslib.org/doc/7004451/

## Role-play, persona, and degeneration (preprints)

Added for [Reconnaissance](docs/17-reconnaissance.md). All identifiers verified.

- **Zhang, X. (2026).** The Curse of Knowledge in Role-Playing Language Models.
  arXiv:2610.17327. 480 scenes with counterfactual twins, 11 open-weight models. Characters use
  content from speech they cannot hear, 67 and 72 percentage points above the guessing baseline.
  Compliance depends on input format; writing as the scene's author leaks nearly as much as
  role-play; giving models only what the character observes stops the leak.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17327
- **Lu, C., Gallagher, J., Michala, J., Fish, K., and Lindsey, J. (2026).** The Assistant Axis:
  Situating and Stabilizing the Default Persona of Language Models. arXiv:2601.10387.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.10387
- **Xu, D., and colleagues (2026).** LoopGuard: Breaking Self-Reinforcing Attention Loops via
  Dynamic KV Cache Intervention. arXiv:2604.10044. Self-reinforcing attention loops, worse in
  smaller models. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10044
- **Unverified:** arXiv:2608.21898, "Training Needs Trustworthy Worlds: Verified Synthetic Web
  Environments for Agent Learning." Appeared in search results, page not retrievable at time of

## Digital media, boredom, and switching

The correction in [The Web as World](docs/14-the-web-as-world.md) rests on these.

- **Tam, K., and colleagues (2024).** Fast-Forward to Boredom: How switching behavior on
  digital media makes people more bored. *Journal of Experimental Psychology: General*. Seven
  experiments, N = 1,223, six preregistered. Digital switching causally **increases** boredom
  and reduces satisfaction, attention, and meaning. https://doi.org/10.1037/xge0001639
- **People are increasingly bored in our digital age (2024).** *Communications Psychology*
  (Nature). Review of converging evidence that digital media use causally increases boredom,
  including X use and within-person boredom, smartphone use at work, and a longitudinal
  result on problematic smartphone use as a predictor of chronic boredom.
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s44271-024-00155-9
- **Boredom and digital media use: A systematic review and meta-analysis (2023).**
  *Computers in Human Behavior Reports*. 59 studies; boredom with digital media use r = .084,
  with problematic use r = .342. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chbr.2023.100313
- **Trait boredom and problematic use of new technologies: a meta-analysis (2026).** 25
  studies, n = 15,152, r = .38. https://doi.org/10.1177/00332941261459995
- **Boredom and smartphone addiction: systematic review and meta-analysis (2025).** 54
  studies, n = 36,245, r = .412. https://doi.org/10.1177/00332941251314713
- **Psychological and physiological effects of applying self-control to the mobile phone
  (2019).** *PLOS One*, preregistered, N = 125. Phone users reported **less** mind-wandering
  (M = 4.20) than participants with no device (M = 6.07, p < .001).
  https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0224464
- **Ophir, E., Nass, C., and Wagner, A. D. (2009).** Cognitive control in media multitaskers.
  *PNAS*, 106(37). Heavy media multitaskers filter worse, both external distractors and
  irrelevant internal representations. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0903620106

## Mind-wandering, spontaneous thought, and the default mode

- **Raichle, M. E., and colleagues (2001).** A default mode of brain function. *PNAS*, 98,
  676 to 682. The resting brain is not idle.
- **Killingsworth, M. A., and Gilbert, D. T. (2010).** A Wandering Mind Is an Unhappy Mind.
  *Science*, 330, 932. 46.9% of waking hours spent mind-wandering; at least 30% during every
  activity except making love. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1192439
- **Christoff, K., Gordon, A. M., Smallwood, J., Smith, R., and Schooler, J. W. (2009).**
  Experience sampling during fMRI reveals default network and executive system contributions
  to mind wandering. *PNAS*, 106, 8719.
- **Christoff, K., Fox, K. C. R., and colleagues (2016).** Mind-wandering as spontaneous
  thought: a dynamic framework. *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*. Spontaneous thought defined by
  absence of strong constraints. https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn.2016.113
- **Smallwood, J., and Schooler, J. W. (2006 / 2015).** The science of mind wandering;
  review of mind-wandering research (*Psychological Bulletin* and later reviews). Perceptual
  decoupling hypothesis.
- **Foster, D. J., and Wilson, M. A. (2006).** Reverse replay of behavioural sequences in
  hippocampal place cells during the awake state. *Nature*. Basis for the replay claims in
  [Sleep](docs/07-sleep-and-consolidation.md).
